# What does residual sugar measure, and is it the same thing as the quality score?

Residual sugar measures the sugar left after fermentation and is recorded in grams per litre [measurements, page 4]. It is not the same thing as the quality score [measurements, page 4]. The notes keep sweetness (residual sugar) and regard (the quality score) apart [examples, page 3], and the quality score is a sensory score given by tasters that is not computed from residual sugar or any other laboratory column [dataset, page 2].
